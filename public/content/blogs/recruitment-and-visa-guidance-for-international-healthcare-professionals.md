## Navigating Your Healthcare Career in Australia 

Australia's healthcare sector has been facing a shortage of skilled professionals for several years, and this trend is expected to continue. Employers are struggling to fill many roles locally, creating a need for overseas workers in healthcare. The same thing is creating opportunities to work in Australia healthcare sector for international nurses, doctors, allied health professionals and other healthcare workers. 

However, getting a healthcare job with visa sponsorship involves more than finding a vacancy that comes with words like “sponsorship available”. Your occupation, qualifications, professional registration, experience, English proficiency and visa eligibility all need to align with the role and employer requirements.

## Employer-Sponsored Visa Options for Healthcare Professionals 

For international healthcare professionals, visa sponsorship may be available through several employer-sponsored pathways. The suitable option depends on the nominated occupation, employer, location, qualifications, experience and visa requirements. Options include Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand visa), Subclass 186 (Employer Nomination Scheme) and Subclass 494 (Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa). 

Some healthcare employers may also use Labour Agreements (LAs) where standard skilled visa programs do not fully meet their workforce needs. ## Understanding Registration, Skills Assessment, and Visa Eligibility 

For international healthcare professionals, professional registration, skills assessment and visa eligibility are separate parts of the Australian migration and employment process. Meeting one does not automatically satisfy for others. 

A skills assessment is an immigration requirement for certain visa pathways and is generally concerned with whether your qualifications and skills meet the requirements for the nominated occupation. Professional registration, on the other hand, determines whether you are permitted to practise a regulated healthcare profession in Australia. 

For example, a healthcare professional may need: 

* Professional registration: AHPRA/National Board assessment and registration requirements 
* Skills assessment: Assessment by the relevant assessing authority where required for the visa 
* Visa eligibility: Immigration requirements for the selected subclass 
* Employment eligibility: Requirements set by the Australian healthcare employer 

Not every healthcare visa pathway requires the same skills assessment because some occupations or applicants may qualify for an exemption.
