## Complete Your AHPRA Nursing Registration 
---
One of the first and most significant steps that overseas applicants will have to take is getting the registration from the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA). AHPRA is the national body that regulates registered health specialists, including nurses and midwives. 

Before you can work as a registered nurse in Australia, it will be necessary for the nurse to fulfil all the requirements for AHPRA registration and submit all necessary documents proving capabilities, identity, and English language skills.

## Prepare Your Professional Documents 
---
Preparing your documents in advance before applying to job openings will make the selection process easier. 

Your checklist must contain documents such as a valid passport, an updated CV according to Australian standards, nursing qualification certificates, current nursing registration, employment references, work experience letters, documents proving your knowledge of English, police clearance and other supporting documents (where applicable).
